# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are UK government leaders across the full spectrum of NewCo maturity: some are still exploring whether a NewCo-style structure fits their department, others are actively establishing, incubating, or scaling one. The community is designed to serve both — early explorers and active builders — rather than a single stage of the journey.

## Product Purpose

The NewCo Community is a practitioner-led forum for UK government leaders working on NewCo-style delivery organisations — bodies structurally separated from their parent department to escape legacy technology, fragmented accountability, and constrained capability. It exists because, until now, there has been no established place for senior leaders to compare approaches, share lessons candidly, and develop practical models for establishing, incubating, and scaling these organisations. The microsite is the front door to that community: it explains what a NewCo is, what the community offers, and how to request to join.

## Positioning

Practitioner-led and candid, not vendor-authored marketing: the community explicitly includes guest articles, practitioner interviews, roundtable summaries, and case studies from across government so the conversation is never solely PA-authored. Content is grounded in real delivery experience — constraints, trade-offs, and organisational dynamics included — not theoretical frameworks. Success for this surface is thought-leadership and brand credibility: establishing PA Consulting as the authoritative convener on NewCo delivery, rather than a direct lead-generation funnel.

## Operating Context

- In-person and hybrid events, run under the Chatham House Rule; every event produces artefacts shared back with the community.
- Diagnostics, canvases, and playbooks that help departments move from theory to action — free to download, evolved with every event.
- The community is convened and facilitated by PA Consulting's NewCos Team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Brand/design system is fixed.** The existing PA Consulting design system (tokens and styles under `_ds/`) is a binding constraint for future work, not a starting point to redesign.
- **Chatham House Rule confidentiality.** Event content and member contributions are shared in confidence; nothing attributable may be published without consent.
- **Invitation-only, manually reviewed access.** Joining is not self-serve. Every request is reviewed by PA's NewCos Team to keep the community practitioner-led and candid, with a stated response time of five working days.
- **Non-PA-authored content commitment.** The site commits to guest articles, interviews, and case studies from across government, not solely PA-authored material — future content work should preserve this mix rather than defaulting to PA-only copy.

## Brand Commitments

Convened by, and attributed to, PA Consulting (PA logo present in brand assets under `uploads/`). The PA Consulting design system already applied to this microsite (`_ds/pa-consulting-design-system-*`) is an existing identity constraint, not a candidate for replacement.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing microsite content (`NewCo Community Microsite.dc.html`) covering: what a NewCo is, why the community exists, event format, guidance/playbooks by NewCo lifecycle stage, thought leadership, community testimonials, and a request-to-join flow.
- Brand assets in `uploads/` (PA Consulting logo, illustrative artwork).
- No testimonials, case studies, benchmarks, or member counts beyond what is already written into the microsite should be fabricated — treat the existing copy as the evidence boundary until real content is supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Practitioner voice over vendor voice — content and design should read as convened-by-PA, not authored-by-PA.
2. Confidentiality is a feature, not friction — the Chatham House Rule and manual review should read as credibility signals, not bureaucratic obstacles.
3. Serve both ends of the maturity spectrum — explorers deciding "should we do this" and builders already scaling a NewCo — without design that implicitly favours one.
4. Brand credibility over conversion — this surface's job is to make PA the obvious convener for NewCo delivery, not to hard-sell engagements.
