A Digital Office should be the engine room of a NewCo — setting technical direction, owning standards and unblocking delivery. In practice, many become approval bottlenecks that recreate the bureaucracy the NewCo was built to escape. This session compared Digital Office patterns from four live transformations.

The strongest consensus of the evening: a Digital Office succeeds when it behaves as a service to delivery teams, with its authority earned through usefulness rather than granted through mandate.
